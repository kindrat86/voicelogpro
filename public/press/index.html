Boilerplate (short)

VoiceLogPro converts voice notes captured on construction and inspection job sites into structured daily logs, so field crews document work in minutes and contractors keep records that hold up when payment or workmanship is disputed.

Boilerplate (long)

VoiceLogPro is a voice-log tool for field and inspection businesses. Crews speak their notes on site; VoiceLogPro structures them into consistent daily logs that capture what happened, when, and where. The product's focus is the record-keeping gap between "I know we did the work" and "I can prove we did the work" — the gap that decides mechanics-lien deadlines and disputed-change-order claims. VoiceLogPro is built by a small independent team and is onboarding founding pilot customers now.
