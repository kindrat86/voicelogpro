## Objection handling

### "We already use Procore, Raken, or another system."

That is fine. This pilot does not ask you to replace it. The crew sends the facts by email, and we return a finished PDF you can review, file, or attach to your existing workflow.

### "The crew will not use another app."

There is no new crew app in this pilot. They can send a voice note, written notes, and optional photos by email.

### "We do not want a subscription."

This is a one-time $49 pilot with no automatic renewal. It does not enroll you in the future Crew Plan.

### "What if the report is inaccurate?"

We generate from the information your team supplies and ask you to review factual accuracy. Missing facts are omitted or shown as not provided. We do not invent weather, times, quantities, or legal conclusions.

### "Is it legally compliant or court-ready?"

We do not guarantee compliance, admissibility, payment, lien rights, or a legal result. The reports are structured documentation intended to support your project records.

### "We are too busy this week."

We can use one active job and one crew. The only daily input is the information you already need to capture, sent as a short voice note or written note with optional photos.
